# Maths functions: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [ESP pre-task: English, maths and digital skills](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-esp-0-core-skills)

## What to do

Four small functions for maths skills. percent_of gives how many out of every 100 (use whole numbers). average adds the numbers and divides by how many. project_cost works out people times days times day rate. minutes_saved takes two lists of minutes and gives back how many minutes the new way saves.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
