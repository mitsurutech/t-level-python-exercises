# Unhappy students: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [6.3: Numbers or words: kinds of data](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-6-3-quantitative-qualitative)

## What to do

Each row of trip feedback is a student, a number of stars from 1 to 5, and a comment. Fill in two functions. The first lists the students who gave 2 stars or fewer. The second finds the lowest score. Both only use the stars: the structured part.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
