# Lapsed customers: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [6.12: Mining, reports and customer data](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-6-12-mining-bi-and-crm)

## What to do

The CRM team wants to write to customers who have stopped buying. Fill in two functions. The first says if one customer has ordered since a date. The second lists every customer who has not.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
