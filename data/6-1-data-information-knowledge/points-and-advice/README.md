# Points and advice: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [6.1: Data, information and knowledge](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-6-1-data-information-knowledge)

## What to do

A football team plays 5 matches. The results are letters: W, D or L. Fill in the three functions. The first turns data into points. The second writes the information as a sentence. The third uses the points to give advice.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
