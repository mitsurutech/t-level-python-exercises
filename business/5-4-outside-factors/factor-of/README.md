# Factor of: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [5.4: Outside factors that force change](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-5-4-outside-factors)

## What to do

Two steps. 1. In the KINDS dictionary, fill in the kind of outside factor for each trigger: "political", "economic", "social", "technological", "legal" or "environmental". Two are done for you. 2. Write the function factor_of. It gets a trigger such as "recession" and gives back its kind from KINDS. For a trigger that is not in KINDS, give back "unknown".

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
