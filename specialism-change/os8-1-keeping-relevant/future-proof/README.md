# Future proof: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [OS 8.1: Keeping a product useful](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-os8-1-keeping-relevant)

## What to do

A delivery app charges a fee of 3.0. Delivery is free when the basket is 20.0 or more. Do not write these numbers inside the functions. Keep them in one settings dictionary. Fill in default_settings, delivery_fee and order_total.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
