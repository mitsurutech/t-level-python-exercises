# Site and 321: Python exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [7.6: Backups, recovery sites and procedures](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-7-6-backup-and-recovery)

## What to do

Write a function. choose_site(hours_allowed) gives back "hot" if the business can wait 1 hour or less, "warm" for up to 24 hours, and "cold" for longer.

## Starter files

- `main.py`

Write your answer in `main.py`. To try it, add a few `print(...)` lines at the bottom and run `python3 main.py`.
